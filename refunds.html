Refunds & cancellation

Last updated: 17 September 2026

No billing is active.

KALKI’s current beta has no active paid plans, checkout, subscription, or recurring charge. You cannot be charged through the beta while billing is disabled, so there is currently no beta purchase to cancel or refund.

Before billing is enabled

The owner will publish the applicable plan prices, taxes, billing timing, cancellation process, refund rules, and any legally required consumer information before enabling checkout. No page or button should be treated as a paid offer until that information and checkout are live.

If you believe you were charged

Keep the transaction date, amount, and payment-provider reference, but do not send a card number, password, one-time code, or other secret. Contact KALKI Support promptly so the owner can investigate. If a payment provider is involved, you may also use its dispute and support process.

Cancellation after launch

Once paid service is actually enabled, the then-current checkout and billing terms will explain how to cancel, when cancellation takes effect, and whether a refund is available. Cancellation will not be assumed from deleting browser data or uninstalling the app unless the paid terms explicitly say so.

Beta data

Stopping beta use does not automatically remove local browser data. To delete it, clear KALKI site data/local storage or uninstall the app. For deletion questions about an authorized connector or service-held data, use Support.
