Support & contact

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Need help with the beta?

Open a support request through the KALKI GitHub issue form. This is the current verified contact route while a dedicated support address is being finalized.

What to include

Describe what you expected, what happened, the page or feature involved, your device/browser, and approximate time. For deletion requests, say which account, connector, or locally stored data you mean. The owner may ask for non-sensitive details to identify the request.

Never include secrets

Do not post passwords, API keys, access tokens, OTPs, recovery codes, payment-card details, private messages, or identity documents. Redact screenshots and logs before attaching them. GitHub issues are public unless the owner provides a private channel.

Connector authorization

KALKI connectors require your explicit authorization and are intended to run only after your explicit action. To disconnect one, first revoke its permission in the provider’s account settings, then tell Support which connector you want removed. Provider-side deletion and retention may require using that provider’s own support or privacy tools.

Beta limitations

This is a limited beta. Billing and checkout are disabled until enabled. Some data is stored only in the browser or device and can be lost when local site data is cleared. AI responses are not guaranteed accurate, and KALKI should not be used as the sole basis for high-impact decisions.

Owner review

These pages are plain-language beta drafts. The owner must review the final legal wording, verified support/contact details, data flows, deletion process, and consumer requirements before a public paid launch. Nothing on this page claims legal compliance.
