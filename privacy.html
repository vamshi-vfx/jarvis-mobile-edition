Security & privacy

Trust starts with clear limits.

This product-status and safety summary is not a security certification. Do not share secrets on this website or in public repository issues.

KALKI is intended to act externally only on explicit user request and approval. WhatsApp, Meta and other third-party connectors are not claimed as live.
