Privacy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Beta scope.

This page describes the current limited KALKI beta, not a promise about a future release. Billing and checkout are disabled until the owner enables them. Please do not put passwords, API keys, payment details, recovery codes, or other secrets into KALKI or beta feedback.

What KALKI may handle

KALKI may handle the prompts, files, settings, chat content, and feedback that you choose to enter. The current browser experience is designed around local-only storage: some information can remain in your browser’s local storage on that device. Local storage is not a backup, is not automatically available on another device, and can be lost when you clear site data, use private browsing, uninstall the app, or your browser resets.

Connectors and providers

A connector is used only after you explicitly authorize it and ask KALKI to use it. Authorization may send information to the relevant provider under that provider’s own terms and privacy policy. KALKI does not claim control over provider retention or security. Review permissions before connecting and revoke access in the provider’s account settings when you no longer need it.

Explicit actions, not automatic monitoring

KALKI is intended to act only after your explicit request. In this beta it is not intended to automatically reply to incoming messages, post to groups, send background messages, or take scheduled actions without your command.

Retention and deletion

To remove locally stored beta data, clear KALKI’s site data/local storage in your browser or uninstall the app. To ask about deletion of information held by a connected provider or by a KALKI beta service, open a request through Support and identify the account or data involved without sending a secret. We will explain what can be removed; provider-side deletion may require using that provider’s own controls.

Accuracy and security limits

AI output can be wrong, incomplete, or unsuitable. Do not rely on it for legal, medical, financial, safety, or other high-impact decisions. No online service or local device storage can be promised completely secure.

Changes

This draft may change as KALKI moves beyond beta. The effective date above will be updated when this page changes.
